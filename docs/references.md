## Discrepancies

- **Cai et al., 2026**: cited in earlier versions for reason-first elicitation; no work matching the author, year, and subject was found on arXiv, Crossref, or the web after three searches, so the sentence now cites Libovický (2026), which reports that direct and chain-of-thought prompting change survey answers.
- **Peng et al., 2026**: the preprint (September 2025) was titled "A mega-study of digital twins reveals strengths, weaknesses and opportunities for further improvement"; the published version carries the title listed. The author list is the published one, 23 authors in Crossref's order; the preprint orders them differently.
- **Okada, Furukawa & Bunji, ACL 2026**: cited in earlier versions for recognising a survey from a few items. The paper compares honest and fake-good instructions on a Big Five inventory and does not test recognition, so the sentence now cites Salecha et al. (2024) alone and the entry was removed.
- **Röttger et al., ACL 2024**: cited in earlier versions for models reproducing test items from memory. The paper does not discuss memorization; it is now cited only for forced and unforced answers and paraphrases.
- **Liu, Panwang & Gu, 2025**: earlier versions gave the size of the shift as "one to three compass units". The article, which is open access, reports regression coefficients "approximately between coefficients of 1 and 3" between versions 0613 and 1106, so the sentence now gives the figure in those terms.
- **Ahn et al., 2026**: v2 (November 2026) changed the title; earlier versions of this file listed "Item-Mean Surrogates: Why Richer Persona Data Fail to Improve LLMs as Human Surrogates".
- **Akimitsu, 2026**: the paper is about an economics exam; it is cited for its appendix on temperature, which reports that OpenRouter accepted `temperature` for OpenAI reasoning models that do not support it.
- **Gao, Liang & Guestrin, ICLR 2025**: the venue is taken from the arXiv listing's comment, "ICLR 2025 Camera-Ready".
- **Helwe, Balalau & Ceolin, ACL 2025** and **Han, Song, Lee & Jo, EACL 2026**: both are in the Findings volumes, not the main proceedings.
- **Kreutner et al., 2025**: the arXiv preprint is December 2025; the published version is EACL 2026 System Demonstrations.
- **Hagendorff, 2025**: the arXiv preprint is 2025; the journal version in AI and Ethics is 2026.
- **Atil et al., 2024**: later arXiv versions carry the title "Non-Determinism of 'Deterministic' LLM Settings"; v1 was "LLM Stability: A detailed analysis with some surprises".
- **Lukauskas & Šarkauskaitė, 2026**: two 2026 preprints by the same authors fit the citation; the entry lists the earlier one, whose text reports endpoint under-use. Its v2 corrects reverse-keyed item scoring and recomputes every number, so a figure taken from v1 may have changed; METHODOLOGY quotes none.
- **He et al., 2025**: the blog post is credited to "Horace He and Thinking Machines Lab", so "et al." stands for the lab, not named co-authors.
